Book update page

Starting a Business After Service

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Veteran, spouse, retiree, entrepreneur helper.

Best first step

Start with SBA VBOC or Boots to Business before paying private coaches.

What to gather

Business idea, target customer, startup cost, income goal and experience.

Safety notes

  • Do not start a business before confirming which veteran-specific loan or certification programs actually apply to your situation.
  • Do not assume a veteran-owned label is granted automatically. It usually requires an application.
  • Do not sign a franchise or lending agreement before checking it against the official SBA veteran resources.
