Retired Military: The First Doors to Know

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Retiree, spouse, annuitant helper.

Best first step

Log into myPay and verify retired pay, contact info and TRICARE timing.

What to gather

Retirement orders, direct deposit, mailing address, DEERS info and spouse contact info.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume retired status means every active-duty resource still applies to you the same way.
  • Do not wait for a paperwork problem to surface before confirming your retiree records are current.
  • Do not skip the retiree-specific in-processing steps at a new installation or region.
