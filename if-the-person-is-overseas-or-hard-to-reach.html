If the Person Is Overseas or Hard to Reach

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Overseas veteran, active-duty family, retiree abroad, spouse, helper.

Best first step

Start with Military OneSource for military-family routing, Red Cross Hero Care for emergency communication and State Department help for U.S. citizen emergencies overseas.

What to gather

Country, city, local phone, passport status, branch or veteran status and the exact concern.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume a bad connection means no one is trying to reach you. Keep the line open on your end too.
  • Do not wait for a scheduled call window if the situation cannot wait.
  • Do not rely on a personal social media message as your main way to reach an official office overseas.
