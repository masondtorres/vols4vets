Lodging, Travel Help and Family Support Near Medical Care

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Family near medical care, caregiver, spouse.

Best first step

Ask the treating facility and Fisher House about lodging options before booking a hotel you cannot afford.

What to gather

Appointment location, dates, patient info, family size and transportation limits.

Safety notes

  • Do not book lodging near a medical facility before confirming the program covers your specific appointment type.
  • Do not assume every family member can stay free. Some lodging programs have their own eligibility rules.
  • Do not wait until the night before an appointment to arrange travel help. Some programs need advance notice.
