Book update page

If a Veteran or Military Family Is Homeless or Close to It

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Veteran, spouse, caregiver, adult child, friend, local helper.

Best first step

Call the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans and ask for the closest official help path.

What to gather

Current location, safe callback number, veteran status, household size, income situation and whether tonight is safe.

Safety notes

  • Do not wait for a shelter bed to be confirmed before you start the call. Start the call first.
  • Do not assume a housing worker already knows this family is at risk. Say it plainly.
  • Do not let embarrassment delay the first call. The programs in this chapter exist for exactly this situation.
