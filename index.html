What this site is, and is not.

What Vols4Vets is

An independent, veteran-owned companion site for the Vols4Vets Veteran Resource Guide. It helps you find the right official door. Based in Sevierville, Tennessee.

What Vols4Vets is not

Not the VA. Not Vets4Vets. Not a crisis line, law firm, medical provider, benefits office or accredited claims representative. Official sources control rules, eligibility, forms, deadlines and decisions.
