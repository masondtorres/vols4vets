Book update page

Education, Tuition Assistance, Credentialing and Career Planning

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Currently serving member, spouse, career helper.

Best first step

Start with your service education office or Military OneSource, then verify branch rules before registering.

What to gather

Branch, degree or credential goal, current orders, deadline and payment question.

Safety notes

  • Do not start a program before checking how it affects any tuition assistance you are already using.
  • Do not assume a school's recruiter speaks for the VA or your service branch.
  • Do not enroll before confirming the school is approved for the benefit you plan to use.
