State Retiree Benefits and Property Tax Starting Points

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Retiree, spouse, state benefit helper.

Best first step

Start with the official state veteran agency before trusting a list.

What to gather

State of residence, DD214, disability letter if relevant, property documents and benefit question.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume a property tax exemption renews on its own. Some states require you to reapply.
  • Do not treat this guide as legal, tax or financial advice. It is not. Use official sources and qualified help.
  • Do not assume the exemption rules from your last state of residence apply in a new one.
