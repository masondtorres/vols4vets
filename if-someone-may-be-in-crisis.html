Book update page

If Someone May Be in Crisis

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Veteran, service member, family, caregiver, friend, pastor, neighbor.

Best first step

Call 988, then press 1, text 838255 or use the official Veterans Crisis Line chat. If there is immediate danger, call 911 or emergency services first.

What to gather

Nothing. Call first. Details can come after.

Safety notes

  • Do not wait for the perfect words before you call. Any call is better than no call.
  • Do not assume someone else already made the call. Confirm it yourself.
  • Do not send crisis details in a casual text. Use the official crisis line instead.
