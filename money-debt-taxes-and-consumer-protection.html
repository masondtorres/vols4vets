Book update page

Money, Debt, Taxes and Consumer Protection

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Service member, veteran, retiree, spouse.

Best first step

Use CFPB, IRS and Military OneSource starting points, then get qualified help when needed.

What to gather

Bills, account statements, tax forms, orders, income and creditor letters.

Safety notes

  • Do not agree to a debt settlement offer before checking it against an official consumer protection source.
  • Do not treat this guide as legal, tax or financial advice. It is not. Use official sources and qualified help.
  • Do not give account numbers to a caller claiming to be a creditor without verifying them first.
