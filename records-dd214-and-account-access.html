Records, DD214 and Account Access

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Transitioning service member, veteran, spouse helper.

Best first step

Learn how DD214 and service record requests work before separation, then store account access safely.

What to gather

Service dates, branch, Social Security number, prior names, contact info and safe storage plan.

Safety notes

  • Do not wait until you need your DD214 to find out where your copy is.
  • Do not assume an old login still works for every VA or DoD account you will need.
  • Do not give your DD214 or account credentials to anyone who is not an official office or a trusted helper you chose.
