Advertising is not advice

Sponsorship, without selling trust.

Vols4Vets can consider three different kinds of listings. Payment never buys a verified badge, a higher editorial rank, a crisis placement, or a claim that this site is the VA.

Three lanes

Free verified resources

Government agencies, veteran service organizations, nonprofits, and useful independent programs. No fee is required to be considered.

Community referrals

Veterans, families, and organizations may recommend a resource. A person reviews it. The organization cannot publish its own listing.

Sponsored partners

A business may ask about a clearly labeled advertisement. Interest is not approval, and this site does not take payment here.

What a sponsor cannot buy

  • Verified status or an official-looking badge.
  • A higher place in editorial lists or search results.
  • Placement on emergency, crisis, or urgent-help routes.
  • Language that says Vols4Vets or the VA recommends the business.
  • A backlink sold to manipulate search rankings.

How a paid placement must look

If a sponsorship is later approved by the owner, the card has to say Sponsored or Advertisement in the placement itself. The link uses rel="sponsored". Editorial pages stay independent. Rates are not published here, and no payment is accepted on this website.

Claims businesses

Help with VA claims is different. Free help should start with a Veterans Service Organization or another VA-accredited representative. Attorneys and claims agents must be VA-accredited. Vols4Vets does not file claims and does not send people to unaccredited claims companies. Accreditation is checked against the official VA search before any listing would be considered: Find a VA-accredited representative.

Emergency routes stay clear

These paths do not take sponsors: urgent help, the Veterans Crisis Line instructions, and pages whose job is to get someone to 911, 988, or homeless veteran support.
