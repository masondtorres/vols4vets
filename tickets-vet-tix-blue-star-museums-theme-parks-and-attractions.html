Tickets: Vet Tix, Blue Star Museums, Theme Parks and Attractions

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Veteran, active-duty family, retiree, local traveler.

Best first step

Use current official pages and never print a static list of attractions as permanent.

What to gather

Status proof, dates, number of tickets, location and transfer rules.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume a Vet Tix ticket is transferable to anyone outside the terms of the program.
  • Do not assume military, veteran, retiree, spouse and dependent all qualify the same way.
  • Do not wait until the day of the event to request tickets. Some programs need lead time.
