Book update page

Established Veteran Service Organizations and Service Officers

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Veteran, survivor, spouse, helper.

Best first step

Use official accredited searches and established organizations, then verify what they can and cannot do.

What to gather

DD214, claim letters, question list and authorization paperwork if needed.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume every Veteran Service Organization handles the same types of claims.
  • Do not sign a power of attorney with a service officer before confirming they are currently accredited.
  • Do not assume membership in an organization is required before you can get help from its service officers.
