PCS, Deployment and Emergency Family Communication

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Active duty, Guard, Reserve, spouse, dependent, parent.

Best first step

For emergency messages use Red Cross Hero Care. For routine PCS and deployment support, start with Military OneSource and installation support.

What to gather

Orders if available, location, dates, family member info and the exact problem.

Safety notes

  • Do not wait for a PCS or deployment date to be confirmed before you set up a family communication plan.
  • Do not assume a spouse or child knows who to call in an emergency. Write the numbers down together.
  • Do not send troop movement or location details in an unsecured text or post.
