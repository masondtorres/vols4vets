Home Loans, Pension, Life Insurance and Burial Benefits

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Veteran, retiree, survivor, spouse helper.

Best first step

Start with VA.gov and the exact VA benefit page before relying on a lender, agent or friend.

What to gather

DD214, income records if relevant, lender papers, insurance papers, death certificate if relevant and VA letters.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume a lender's advertised veteran rate is the same as the VA's official loan guaranty terms.
  • Do not wait until a policy has lapsed to check your life insurance coverage.
  • Do not assume burial benefits are automatic. Confirm what must be requested and by whom.
