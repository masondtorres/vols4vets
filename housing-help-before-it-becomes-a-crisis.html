Housing Help Before It Becomes a Crisis

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Veteran, spouse, caregiver, local helper.

Best first step

Call the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans or local 211 before the last safe night.

What to gather

Address, eviction or foreclosure notice, income, household size and veteran status.

Safety notes

  • Do not wait for an eviction notice or a final unpaid bill before reaching out. Call while there is still time to work with a program.
  • Do not assume a shelter or rental assistance program already knows your situation. Say it plainly on the first call.
  • Do not send financial or housing details in a casual text. Use the official help route instead.
