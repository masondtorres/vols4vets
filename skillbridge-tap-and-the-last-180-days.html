SkillBridge, TAP and the Last 180 Days

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Separating active-duty member, retiree, spouse helper.

Best first step

Talk to your command and installation SkillBridge or TAP office before applying anywhere.

What to gather

Separation date, command approval path, desired program and TAP status.

Safety notes

  • Do not wait until your last weeks in uniform to start your transition paperwork.
  • Do not assume SkillBridge and TAP cover the same ground. Check what each one actually requires.
  • Do not skip the counseling step because it feels like a formality. It is where eligibility gets confirmed.
