Veterans Day Deals: How to Use the Annual List Without Getting Burned

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Veteran, family, helper, website editor.

Best first step

Use the Vols4Vets website for the current-year list and check each company page before going.

What to gather

Current year, location, status proof, restaurant or store terms and time window.

Safety notes

  • Do not shop from last year's Veterans Day deals list. Confirm the current year's offers directly.
  • Do not assume every store on the list participates at every location.
  • Do not buy before checking the exclusions, dates, locations and proof you will need.
