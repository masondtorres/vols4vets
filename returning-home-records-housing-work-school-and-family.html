Returning Home: Records, Housing, Work, School and Family

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Veteran returning from overseas, spouse, adult child.

Best first step

Use VA.gov, NARA, state veteran agency and local resources in the destination state.

What to gather

DD214, address, school records, job plan, insurance status and local support contacts.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume your records, housing search and job search can all wait until after you settle in. Some steps need to start before you arrive.
  • Do not promise a landlord, employer or school a start date before your paperwork is confirmed.
  • Do not wait until a deadline, bill, move date or appointment is nearly here. Start while there is still room to fix it.
