Book update page

Moving, Housing, Family and School Planning

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Transitioning family, spouse, parent, adult child.

Best first step

Build a move file and verify official routes for housing, school and family support before terminal leave.

What to gather

Orders, family size, school records, housing budget, destination and medical needs.

Safety notes

  • Do not sign a lease or enroll a child in school before confirming the move date is final.
  • Do not assume a school district's boundary lines are the same as they were last year.
  • Do not skip a housing walkthrough checklist because the move feels rushed.
