Contact Us

Email Vols4Vets

We are a small veteran-owned guide. We help people find official starting points. We are not the VA, not a help desk and not an emergency service.

This is not a crisis line.

If you or someone you know is in crisis, call 988 and press 1 or text 838255. For immediate danger, call 911. Do not email us for emergencies.

What you can email us about

  • A correction or wrong link on the site
  • A resource suggestion or nonprofit listing request
  • General questions about the guide
  • Feedback on the website

Do not send Social Security numbers, claim numbers, medical records, passwords or other private documents.

How to reach us

We prefer email only. We do not accept telephone calls from website visitors.

Click the button below to open your email app with our address. The address is built in your browser so it is not left in plain text for scrapers.
