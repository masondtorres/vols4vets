Caregivers and the VA Caregiver Path

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Caregiver, spouse, adult child, veteran.

Best first step

Call the VA Caregiver Support Line and ask what first step fits the situation.

What to gather

Veteran info, caregiver relationship, care needs, VA enrollment status and current providers.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume caregiver status is automatic once you start providing care. It must be applied for.
  • Do not wait for burnout before looking into respite care and caregiver support options.
  • Do not assume every caregiver program has the same eligibility rules as the VA Caregiver Support Program.

Call the Caregiver Support Line or use VA caregiver resources

Official website: https://www.caregiver.va.gov/

What to gather: Veteran info, caregiver role, care needs, VA care team if known

VA Health Care Enrollment

Apply online at VA.gov

Phone: 877-222-8387
Official website: https://www.va.gov/health-care/how-to-apply/

What to gather: DD214, Social Security number, income information if applicable, current health insurance information
