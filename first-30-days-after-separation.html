Book update page

First 30 Days After Separation

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

New veteran, spouse, helper.

Best first step

Confirm VA.gov access, records storage, health coverage status and local support before the first crisis.

What to gather

DD214, VA.gov login, insurance status, housing address, job plan and claim or appointment notices.

Safety notes

  • Do not go 30 days without a plan for health coverage. Gaps are hard to fix after the fact.
  • Do not assume your last paycheck and your first civilian paycheck will overlap without a gap.
  • Do not wait for a problem to appear before setting up your VA.gov account.
