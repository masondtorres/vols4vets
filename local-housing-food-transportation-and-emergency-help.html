Book update page

Local Housing, Food, Transportation and Emergency Help

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

East Tennessee veteran, family, local helper.

Best first step

Use 211, the county VSO and official local providers, then verify each provider before sending someone.

What to gather

ZIP code, need type, transportation limits, household size and urgent safety concerns.

Safety notes

  • Do not wait for a shelter bed or food bank slot to open before you start the call.
  • Do not assume a local office already knows about a family in crisis. Say it plainly.
  • Do not send financial or housing details in a casual text. Use the official local help line instead.
