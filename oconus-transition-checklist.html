Book update page

OCONUS Transition Checklist

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Transitioning member overseas, spouse, family helper.

Best first step

Build the transition calendar early and verify TAP, records, medical and move routes through official offices.

What to gather

Separation date, orders, passports, records, housing destination, school records and medical needs.

Safety notes

  • Do not start an overseas move without confirming which of your records need to be updated before you leave.
  • Do not assume a stateside process, like a benefits claim, pauses cleanly while you are OCONUS. Some steps still have deadlines.
  • Do not wait until you land to figure out where your nearest support resources are.
