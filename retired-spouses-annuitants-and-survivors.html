Book update page

Retired Spouses, Annuitants and Survivors

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Spouse, annuitant, survivor, adult child.

Best first step

Start with DFAS, VA survivor pages and the family document file.

What to gather

Death certificate if relevant, marriage records, SBP info, DD214 and pay account details.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume a surviving spouse automatically keeps every benefit the retiree had.
  • Do not wait for an ID card to expire before renewing it as a survivor or dependent.
  • Do not assume annuitant paperwork transfers automatically without a direct check with DFAS.

Start with DFAS retired and annuitant pay pages or myPay
