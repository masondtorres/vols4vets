About Vols4Vets

Companion site for the Vols4Vets Veteran Resource Guide.

The printed book is the stable path. This site keeps official links, phone numbers and chapter updates easier to check. Vols4Vets is veteran-owned in Sevierville and is not the VA.

Founders

Mason and Jen Torres

Mason and Jen are United States Air Force veterans. They have lived in East Tennessee since 1998 and raised their family here.

The guide exists so a veteran or family member can find the next official door without wading through clutter.

Local roots

Independent. Veteran-owned. Based in Sevierville, Tennessee. Name is Vols4Vets, not Vets4Vets.

What this site does

It points you to the right starting place.

Find resources

Benefits, health care, crisis support, jobs, housing, discounts and family support.

Start local

Sevier County, Knoxville, Mountain Home VA and East Tennessee offices.

Check the source

Use official offices and providers before you drive, apply, call or make plans.

What Vols4Vets is

A small veteran-owned companion site for the Vols4Vets Veteran Resource Guide.

What Vols4Vets is not

Not the VA. Not a crisis line. Not a law firm. Not an accredited claims representative. For immediate danger call 911. For crisis support call 988 and press 1.
