Federal Jobs, USAJOBS and Veterans Preference

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Veteran, transitioning member, spouse helper.

Best first step

Create a USAJOBS profile and read each announcement line by line.

What to gather

Federal resume, DD214, preference documents, transcripts and disability letter if relevant.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume veterans preference applies automatically without the correct documentation attached to your application.
  • Do not submit a federal application without confirming the current USAJOBS requirements for that specific posting.
  • Do not wait until a hiring window closes to gather your DD214 and preference paperwork.
