Sevier County

Veteran resources for Sevier County.

A local routing page for veterans in Sevierville, Pigeon Forge, Gatlinburg, Kodak, Seymour and nearby communities.

Fast answer: Use official county, state and VA sources first. If you need a county-specific benefit or local document help, check the official county office page before you drive or call.

Who this page is for

Sevier County veterans and family members trying to sort out whether a problem belongs with VA, Tennessee state veterans services, a county office, workforce help or legal aid.

First three steps

  1. Write down your county, deadline and the exact problem.
  2. Use the official source links below to find the right office.
  3. Ask what documents are needed before sending or bringing records.

Before you call or drive

  • Check the official page.
  • Confirm hours.
  • Confirm appointment rules.
  • Ask what documents to bring.
  • Ask whether copies are accepted.
  • Ask whether the office handles your exact issue.
  • Do not send private records to Vols4Vets.

What to bring

Photo ID, DD214 if available, proof of Sevier County address if requested, VA letters, income documents for need-based programs and housing or court notices if relevant.

What not to send to Vols4Vets

Do not send Social Security numbers, claim numbers, DD214s, medical records, legal papers, passwords or private case details.

What happens after this link

  • You may leave Vols4Vets.
  • The official site may ask for private information.
  • Only enter private records on official secure sites or with verified accredited help.
  • Write down confirmation numbers.
  • Save copies of what you submit.
  • Come back to Vols4Vets if you need the next starting point.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming one local office handles every veteran issue.
  • Waiting until eviction, appeal or application deadlines are too close.
  • Using an unverified phone number from an old search result.

When to use urgent or crisis help

Use 911 for immediate danger. Use 988 then press 1 or text 838255 for veteran crisis support. Use VA homeless veteran support if you are homeless or may lose housing soon.
