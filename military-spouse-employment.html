Military Spouse Employment

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Military spouse, active-duty family, employer helper.

Best first step

Start with the Military Spouse Employment Partnership and Military OneSource spouse resources.

What to gather

Resume, location, remote work needs, sponsor status and timeline.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume every state honors an occupational license the same way as the state where it was issued.
  • Do not wait until after a PCS to start a spouse employment or licensing transfer request.
  • Do not assume a program name is the same nationwide. Confirm what is available at your specific installation or state.
