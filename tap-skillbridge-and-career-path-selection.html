Book update page

TAP, SkillBridge and Career Path Selection

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Transitioning service member, spouse, career helper.

Best first step

Use TAP for the required base plan and SkillBridge only after command and eligibility checks.

What to gather

Separation date, resume draft, target location, skills, command route and training goals.

Safety notes

  • Do not pick a career path before comparing what TAP and SkillBridge each actually offer for it.
  • Do not assume a certification will transfer to a civilian license without checking the specific state's rule.
  • Do not wait until separation to start researching the path. Start while you still have time to change course.
