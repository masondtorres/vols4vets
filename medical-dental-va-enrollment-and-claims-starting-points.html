Book update page

Medical, Dental, VA Enrollment and Claims Starting Points

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Transitioning service member, veteran, spouse helper.

Best first step

Use TAP, VA health care enrollment and official VA claims starting points. Get accredited help for claim questions.

What to gather

Medical records, service treatment records, separation date, current insurance and VA.gov account access.

Safety notes

  • Do not put off VA enrollment because you are still deciding whether to file a claim. The two steps are separate.
  • Do not guess at claims language. Use VA or an accredited helper.
  • Do not assume a dental or medical benefit works the same way it did while you were on active duty.
