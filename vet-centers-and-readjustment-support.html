Vet Centers and Readjustment Support

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Veteran, service member, family, caregiver.

Best first step

Use VA Find Locations to find current Vet Center and readjustment counseling options.

What to gather

ZIP code, service history, concern type and whether family support is needed.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume a Vet Center only serves veterans who served in combat.
  • Do not wait for a crisis before making first contact with a Vet Center.
  • Do not assume Vet Center visits will appear on the same records as VA medical center visits.
