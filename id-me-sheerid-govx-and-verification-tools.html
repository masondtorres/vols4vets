ID.me, SheerID, GovX and Verification Tools

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Shopper, veteran, active duty, spouse helper.

Best first step

Read both the verification tool instructions and the company offer terms.

What to gather

Photo ID, email, phone, status proof and privacy comfort level.

Safety notes

  • Do not enter personal information into a verification site before confirming it is the official ID.me, SheerID or GovX page.
  • Do not assume one verification service works everywhere. Some retailers only accept a specific one.
  • Do not wait until checkout to start the verification process. Some take time to approve.
