Book update page

Survivors, Dependents and Gold Star Families

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Survivor, dependent, Gold Star family, helper.

Best first step

Use VA survivor pages, TAPS for support and accredited help for benefit questions.

What to gather

Death certificate, DD214, marriage or dependency records, VA letters and safe contact info.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume survivor and dependent benefits start automatically without an application.
  • Do not wait for a Gold Star family liaison to reach out first. You can make first contact.
  • Do not assume a benefit available to one type of survivor applies the same way to another.
