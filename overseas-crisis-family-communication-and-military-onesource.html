Overseas Crisis, Family Communication and Military OneSource

This Vols4Vets.com page keeps current links, phone numbers and source notes for the book chapter.

Official-source caution: Vols4Vets.com is a routing companion to the book. Official agencies, crisis lines, providers and program pages control action, eligibility, deadlines, phone numbers and forms.

Who this helps

Overseas service member, veteran abroad, family helper.

Best first step

Use Military OneSource OCONUS routes, Red Cross Hero Care and State Department emergency pages.

What to gather

Country, city, local contact, branch or veteran status, emergency facts and passport status.

Safety notes

  • Do not assume Military OneSource only helps with paperwork. It also supports overseas family crises.
  • Do not wait for a scheduled call window if the family's situation cannot wait.
  • Do not send crisis details in a casual text or social post. Use the official crisis line instead.
