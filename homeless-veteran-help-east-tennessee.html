Housing crisis

Homeless veteran help in East Tennessee.

If housing is unstable, start with urgent official support. Do not wait for every record to be perfect before you call.

Fast answer: If there is immediate danger, call 911. For veteran crisis support, call 988 then press 1 or text 838255. For homeless veteran support, call the VA National Call Center for Homeless Veterans at 877-424-3838.

Who this page is for

Veterans and helpers in East Tennessee who are homeless, staying somewhere temporary, facing eviction, living in a vehicle or worried housing may be lost soon.

First three steps

  1. Use 911 first if there is immediate danger.
  2. Call VA homeless veteran support at 877-424-3838 if the person is homeless or at risk of homelessness.
  3. Write down county, safe callback option, deadline, pets or dependents and any immediate safety issue.

Official housing programs to ask about

Ask the VA homeless line about HUD-VASH, SSVF, Grant and Per Diem, and Health Care for Homeless Veterans. These are VA programs. Vols4Vets does not place people in housing.

Knox County Veterans Services lists a VA Homeless Outreach Office number of 865-670-2369 and Tennessee Valley Coalition to End Homelessness at 888-556-0791 on its helpful-numbers page. Confirm those numbers on the official county page before you rely on them.

Before you call or drive

  • Check the official page.
  • Confirm hours.
  • Confirm appointment rules.
  • Ask what documents to bring.
  • Do not send private records to Vols4Vets.

What to prepare before calling

County, current sleeping situation, immediate safety concerns, safe contact method, eviction or utility deadline, ID status and whether DD214 is available. Do not delay the call if records are missing.

Safety and privacy note

Vols4Vets is not an emergency service, shelter provider, law firm or case manager. Do not send eviction papers, Social Security numbers, claim numbers, DD214s, medical records, legal papers, passwords or private case details to Vols4Vets.
